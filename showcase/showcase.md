# Fathom

A little light. A very hungry ocean. You are a glowing forager in a flooded maze,
with nothing but a pocket of cold light between you and the abyss. Graze plankton
to brighten the water around you, learn the corridors, and find a way through the
dark.

Your light remembers where you have been—but only a circular window lets you see
those memories. Eat and the window grows. Stop feeding and the world closes in.
Send a cyan sonar pulse around the next bend to map an escape, or save your
silence for whatever might be listening.

Three predators hunt by different senses. The Lanternjaw follows your light,
wearing the same amber glow as a harmless drifter. The eyeless Gloamfin follows
sound; sharp turns can cost it the speed it needs to catch you. The Flarefish
briefly sets the trench ablaze, showing you the maze even as it searches for you.
Ink can hide your light. It cannot hide your noise.

Eat the last mote and descend. Each deeper maze brings more hunters and a shorter
sonar reach, while your reserves dwindle with every catch. Learn when to shine,
when to listen, and when to disappear.
